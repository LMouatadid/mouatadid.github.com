Research

My thesis, Algebraic codes from skew and twisted structures, was supervised by Pr Mustapha Najmeddine at ENSAM Meknès. It covers monomial and quasi-T-cyclic codes, twisted codes over metacyclic groups, and dual codes arising from 2-cocycles.

Keywords: coding theory, skew polynomial rings, twisted group algebras, Galois LCD and self-orthogonal codes, quantum codes.
